About this tool

Built by LAAPOA for LAXPD officers to submit Watch and RDO bids.

Your data

  • Your name, serial, rank, and watch are saved on this device only (browser localStorage) so you don't have to retype them each time. Clear them anytime via the "Forget saved info" option in the form.
  • Your bid selections never leave your device until you generate the PDF and email it yourself.
  • Aggregate page visits are tracked via Cloudflare Web Analytics — no cookies, no personal data, no fingerprinting.
